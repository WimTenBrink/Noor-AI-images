# Senna Bakker: Investigative Music Journalist & Reporter

## At a Glance
- **Age:** 21
- **Role:** Senior Lead Reporter at *Dutch Noise Chronicles Magazine*
- **Status:** Single & Asexual
- **Obsession:** High-performance sports motorbikes & raw, authentic songwriting
- **Vibe:** Sharp, fiercely independent, and armed with an espresso-fueled intellect

---

## Biographical Profile

Senna Bakker is not your average music reporter. At just 21 years old, she has already earned a reputation as one of the most respected, sharp-witted, and relentless journalists in the European indie and alternative music scenes. Born in Groningen and raised in Utrecht, Senna bypassed the traditional academic routes, having already published her first major investigative piece on underground festival safety at the age of 17. 

Known for her iconic leather jacket, silver ear cuffs, and the low rumble of her beloved customized matte-black **Ducati Panigale V4** (which she drives at blistering speeds across the Dutch polders), Senna is a force of nature. She is completely single, proudly asexual, and dedicates 100% of her kinetic energy to unearthing the raw truth behind the music.

For Senna, interviewing artists isn't about PR fluff; it's about dissecting the philosophy, the friction, and the creative spirit. It is through her sharp, immersive columns at *Dutch Noise Chronicles* that the public has gained unparalleled access to the inner lives of bands like *Noor*.

---

## The Noor Connection

Senna is the exclusive journalist entrusted with interviewing the four singers of Noor. Because of the band's fiercely guarded privacy at their Abcoude farm studio, they rarely speak to outsiders. However, they made an exception for Senna. They respect her absolute integrity, her lack of romantic/male-centric bias, and her shared commitment to celebrating queer and female artistry.

Whenever Noor releases a new song, Senna rides her sports bike down the winding roads of Abcoude, parking right outside the converted Sound Studio Barn. She sits with Miranda, Annelies, Fannie, and Emma on the living room rugs or around the Norse Pagan Altar, asking the deep, probing, and sometimes mischievous questions that form the band's famous Post-Song Interviews.

## Professional Philosophy

> "If your music has double entendres, I will find them. If you hid a secret chord progression, I will hear it. And if you are larping for the charts, I will write about it."
> — *Senna Bakker*

Senna's interviewing style is a blend of intense musicological knowledge and warm, disarming humor. She writes with a vintage fountain pen in leather-bound notebooks, refusing to record audio unless strictly necessary, trusting her highly trained auditory memory. Her profiles are legendary for their literary quality, capturing the rustle of bare feet on barn floorboards, the tension of creative disagreement, and the silent, romantic glances shared between band members.
